Investment Scam: The Signs of an Online Investment Scam

An investment scam is a deposit into a story. The signs of an online investment scam are stable: someone found you, the profit is on their screen, and your money leaves only after a new fee. We investigate losses as a cybersecurity company — we do not replace the bank, the card scheme, or the police.

The signs

Signs of an online investment scam

  • Contact started with a call, a WhatsApp, a dating app or an ad you did not search for.
  • The platform was opened for you. You did not type the domain yourself.
  • Returns are promised as a number, not as a market that can go down.
  • A withdrawal is approved, then a tax or a margin call appears.
  • The 'regulator' badge does not resolve on the public register.
Why people miss it

The first week is designed to look ordinary

Investment scams copy real broker language. The split is not the logo. The split is whether you can leave without paying again, and whether the firm exists on a register you typed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are the signs of an online investment scam?

Cold contact, a domain you did not type, a return that cannot fall, and a withdrawal that needs a new payment. One of those is enough to treat the file as a complaint.

The company has a licence PDF. Is that enough?

No. Open the regulator's public register yourself. A PDF on their site is not the register.
