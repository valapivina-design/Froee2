Investigation vs a Recovery Firm

An investigation writes a file. A recovery firm sells an outcome. If a stranger found you after a loss and asks for a fee to start, that is the second pitch. We investigate losses as a cybersecurity company — we do not replace the bank, the card scheme, or the police.

The tell

How a second pitch sounds

  • They wrote you first. You did not find them.
  • They already 'traced' the money before you sent a date.
  • They want a fee, a tax or a wallet connect to begin.
  • They tell you not to call the bank or the police.
  • They promise a percentage or a timeline.
Our line

What a cybersecurity company does instead

We investigate losses. We ask for the date and how you paid. We do not replace the bank, the card scheme or the police. The first look at the file is free. That is the whole offer.
