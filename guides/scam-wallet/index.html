Scam Wallet: How a Fake Crypto Wallet Takes the Keys

A scam wallet looks like a normal app. A fake crypto wallet can copy a known name, ask for a seed phrase, or show a balance that is not on the chain. The payment still left a trail. We investigate losses as a cybersecurity company — we do not replace the bank, the card scheme, or the police.

Red flags

Signs the wallet is the product, not the tool

  • The app was installed from a link in a chat, not from the store page you typed.
  • Someone asked you to 'import' or 'validate' a seed phrase on a website.
  • The balance grows on screen and every withdrawal needs a new fee send.
  • Support only exists inside the same chat that sold the wallet.
  • The app name differs from the official wallet by one letter.
Two different events

Drained wallet versus fake-wallet theatre

A real wallet can be drained if the seed left the device. That is a theft of keys. A fake crypto wallet never held keys you controlled — the balance was a picture.

Both still start with the date and how you paid. Send the date and how you paid. The first look at the file is free.

Fake crypto wallet or a real app you mistyped

  • You opened the store yourself.You installed an APK or a TestFlight link from a stranger.
  • The seed stays off the internet.A page asked you to type 12 or 24 words.
  • A withdrawal goes to an address you chose.A withdrawal goes to an address the app chose, after a fee.
What to send

The wallet file

The app name and the URL or store link you used

One character is enough to separate a clone from the real wallet.

The transaction

If you sent coins in, that tx is the investigation. If you only typed a seed, say so — that is a different file.

Do not type the seed here

Never send a seed phrase by form, email or chat. If it was typed into a fake page, treat those keys as burned.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a scam wallet?

An app or a page that shows a balance or asks for a seed so that a payment or the keys leave your control. It is not a real wallet you hold.

I already typed my seed. What now?

Do not send that seed to anyone who offers to 'restore' it. Move any coins that remain to new keys you created yourself. Then send the date and the fake URL — not the words.

Can you unlock the fake app?

No. We do not hold that account. We investigate the payment and the page you used.
