Fund Recovery Services: How to Get Money Back From a Scam Broker — Without Getting Scammed Twice

Search “fund recovery services” and half the results are the second scam: fake recovery agents who prey on people the first scam already bled. Real recovery is quieter, slower and evidence-driven. Here is how the legitimate routes actually work — and how to tell them apart from the predators.

Know the landscape

How to get money back from a forex broker: the three real routes

  • Chargeback for investment fraud: if you paid by card or bank transfer, your bank can dispute the transaction — but the window is short and the evidence bar is real.
  • Crypto tracing: stolen Bitcoin or USDT moves on a public ledger; a forensic trace follows it to the exchange where it is cashed out and freezes the exit.
  • Regulatory and legal pressure: complaints to the right regulator, police reports and — where amounts justify it — civil claims against operators, payment processors and mules.
The honest mechanics

How fund recovery services actually work

No honest investigator can promise a number, because recovery depends on how you paid, how long ago, and where the money went. What a professional service does promise is process: preserve the evidence, identify the money route, and apply the pressure that route responds to — a bank dispute, an exchange seizure request, a regulator freeze or a court order.

Success looks unglamorous: a recall approved because the evidence pack was complete; an exchange account frozen because the blockchain trail was documented; a settlement because a payment processor faced liability. Each starts with the same forensic file.

Protect yourself

Recovery-scam red flags: the second trap

  • Guaranteed recovery, upfront.Anyone promising a percentage recovered “or your fee back” before seeing your case is selling hope, not work.
  • They contacted you first.Legitimate firms do not DM victims under pseudonyms or find you in comment sections.
  • Payment in crypto, fee before work.A “registration fee”, “court filing fee” or “wallet verification” paid in advance to unlock your recovery is the advance-fee scam wearing a rescue uniform.
  • No verifiable identity.A real firm has a registered company, named investigators and checkable history — not a Telegram handle and a stock-photo team page.
The routes, in detail

Chargeback, crypto tracing and court: what each can really do

Chargeback for investment deposits

Card networks and banks allow disputes on transactions that were not as described — and a broker that refuses withdrawal after taking a deposit fits that definition. Deadlines run from weeks to a few months depending on the payment rail. The bank needs a clean file: receipts, the platform's terms, your withdrawal requests and their refusals.

Can you recover stolen crypto?

Often, partly — and faster than people think, if you move quickly. The transfer is permanent but public; professional tracing links your coins to the exchange accounts where they are sold, and exchanges freeze accounts on credible legal process. Once cashed out through mule accounts, the route narrows — which is why the first 72 hours matter more than the first three months.

When courts and regulators enter

Large losses justify more than disputes: regulator complaints can freeze an operation's banking, and civil claims can reach the payment processors and individuals who handled the money. This is slow and document-heavy — exactly the kind of work a specialist investigation firm exists to do.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are fund recovery services legitimate — and how do you choose the best fund recovery companies 2026?

Some are — and the legitimate ones share a profile: registered company, named investigators, verifiable track record, fees tied to work performed, and an honest assessment before any engagement. The industry also has a large layer of fraud aimed at previous victims, so verification comes before trust.

Can you recover stolen crypto after months?

Possibly. Blockchain records do not expire, and exchange records are retained under KYC law. Old cases are harder — funds may have been cashed out through layered accounts — but tracing them still produces the documented evidence that regulators and courts act on.

What does a real recovery assessment cost?

A credible initial assessment is free, because the firm cannot price work it has not scoped. Be sceptical of any “mandatory” advance fee — especially one payable in crypto — before a single hour of work has been done on your file.
