FundedNext Review — Is FundedNext a Scam?

FundedNext is FundedNext (fundednext.com) — a UAE-marketed prop-evaluation brand (contracting entity GrowthNext – F.Z.E., Ajman Free Zone) selling Stellar CFD and futures challenge accounts on simulated capital. Challenge fees buy a contract product; “funded” stages are not FCA/ASIC client-money protection. What is documented: Trustpilot https://www.trustpilot.com/review/fundednext.com: ~84,000 reviews, TrustScore about 4.5 / 5 (~82% five-star / ~8% one-star as of mid-November 2026). The firm invites customers to review and had replied to 0% of negative reviews in that snapshot — useful signal, not a regulator finding. You are not alone, and it is not your fault.

The short answer

The short answer: a real prop brand — and a live file of post-pass compliance kills and cancelled payouts

We do not need rumours to say that. FundedNext's own published material and the documented complaint record give it away:

  • Trustpilot https://www.trustpilot.com/review/fundednext.com: ~84,000 reviews, TrustScore about 4.5 / 5 (~82% five-star / ~8% one-star as of mid-November 2026). The firm invites customers to review and had replied to 0% of negative reviews in that snapshot — useful signal, not a regulator finding.
  • Company / terms: services contracted with GrowthNext – F.Z.E. (Ajman Free Zone, UAE). FundedNext’s own help materials state it “operates under a distinctive framework and is not subject to conventional financial regulations.” Challenge fees and reward payouts sit under its terms — not FOS/FSCS-style cover.
  • Complaint pattern (Trustpilot one-stars, LinkedIn, prop-dispute write-ups): traders allege they passed published profit and loss limits, then hit a mandatory “Compliance Session,” account termination citing IP discrepancy or “trading activity” without a specific rule breach pack, or cancelled multi-thousand-dollar reward requests after compliance review — allegations and contract disputes, not criminal convictions.
  • Related brand hygiene: FNmarkets is a separate CFD brokerage in the same founder ecosystem with offshore licence claims (e.g. Comoros / Mauritius style registrations in public commentary). Opening FNmarkets is optional per FundedNext’s help centre — do not conflate a prop challenge dispute with a regulated retail brokerage deposit. Also do not confuse fundednext.com with nextgen-funded-binary.com (a different, flagged brand).
  • Anyone who offers to recover a FundedNext challenge fee or “denied payout” for an upfront fee, or who asks for a seed phrase, a password, or a 2FA code, is attempting a second theft.
