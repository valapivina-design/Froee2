Pip Kings Review — Is Pip Kings a Scam?

Pip Kings is a UK forex-signals and auto-copy trading service run by named individuals (reviewers cite “Ryan Hancock” and “Ben”) whose Trustpilot has sat at 1.7/5 for years — reviewers allege blown copy accounts and a £500 “guaranteed funded account” that never existed. What is documented: Trustpilot 1.7/5 “Bad”, sustained 1-star reviews from 2021 through 2025: “Absolute scam artist” (24 Oct 2024, £500 taken for a “guaranteed funded account” the reviewer believes never existed); “AVOID THIS SCAM” (2022); “Scam” (2022). The short answer

The short answer: Pip Kings exhibits the core red flags of a withdrawal-block operation

We do not need rumours to say that. Pip Kings's own published material and the documented complaint record give it away:

  • Trustpilot 1.7/5 “Bad”, sustained 1-star reviews from 2021 through 2025: “Absolute scam artist” (24 Oct 2024, £500 taken for a “guaranteed funded account” the reviewer believes never existed); “AVOID THIS SCAM” (2022); “Scam” (2022).
  • Auto-copy losses: reviewers describe accounts blown by over-leveraging on the copy service — “they over leveraged until everyone’s accounts got blown” (~£1,000 lost, 2020); another reports £3,000–£4,500 lost on autocopy (2021–2022).
  • Alleged review buying: “this company pays for reviews… more than 8 five star reviews the same day” (10 Oct 2021) — a claim we attribute to the reviewer.
  • The “elite package” (~£1,000+) coaching is described by a buyer as near-contentless — two chart screenshots’ worth of advice, mentor repeatedly no-showing (5 Dec 2021).
  • Refund stonewalling: a reviewer promised a £500 refund on 4 October 2024 describes being gaslit and ignored “to the point where I cannot pay my rent this month” (24 Oct 2024). Defamation note: the individuals named above are identified by reviewers; the allegations are theirs, and we report them as allegations.
What victims report

None of this resolves by paying more. And if it matches what happened to you, you are not alone — this is exactly the caseload our investigators handle every week.
