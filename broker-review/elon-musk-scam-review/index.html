Non-defamation notice: Elon Musk is a real, respected public figure with no connection to the schemes described on this page. Nothing here alleges any wrongdoing by Elon Musk — the only criminals are the impersonators, and this page exists to protect both their victims and the person whose identity is being stolen.

What checks out about the real person:

  • Elon Musk is the real, publicly documented chief executive of Tesla, SpaceX, X and xAI — one of the most covered individuals on earth, with no involvement in any consumer trading platform or crypto giveaway.

What the impersonation fraud looks like — every item below is documented:

  • The Ontario Securities Commission's investor warning of 30 Mar 2026 names “Elon Musk AI Trading” directly (domain elonmuskaitrading.com via IOSCO I-SCAN) as unregistered to deal or advise in Ontario.
  • Hong Kong's SFC warned on 8 May 2024 that deepfake Elon Musk videos were promoting the Quantum AI trading scam, and police were asked to block the sites.
