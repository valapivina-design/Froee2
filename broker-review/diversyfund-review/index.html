DiversyFund Review — Is DiversyFund a Scam?

DiversyFund is DiversyFund (diversyfund.com / often misspelled “Diversify Fund”) — a US multifamily crowdfunding sponsor whose retail vehicle DF Growth REIT II, LLC sold $10 Class A shares under Regulation A; that fund’s offering is permanently suspended, capital is still inside the vehicle, and there is no working redemption exit. What is documented: DF Growth REIT II, LLC (Delaware; EDGAR filer; manager DF Manager, LLC; sponsor DiversyFund, Inc.): Regulation A Class A Investor Shares at $10. Offering circular qualified 29 January 2021; company filings state roughly $11,346,778 raised (1,134,678 shares). Temporary Reg A suspension January 2022; permanent suspension by settled SEC order 9 June 2023 (Securities Act Release 33-11204) — technical Reg A timing/volume breaches and inaccurate website statements alleged; no fine and no fraud finding in that settlement. Staff later said it would not recommend further enforcement against DiversyFund, REIT I, REIT II, DF Manager, or named principals (Craig Cecilio / Alan Lewis). You are not alone, and it is not your fault.

What else the record shows:

  • Liquidity for REIT II holders: filings and independent summaries describe no redemption programme and no secondary market. Capital return depends on asset sales in wind-up — not a dashboard “withdraw” button. A September 2025 Form 1-U moved dissolution to 31 December 2026; a 30 September 2026 Form 1-U (accession 0001213900-26-104852) moved it again to 31 December 2027 (second/final discretionary year under the LLC agreement), with management saying it wants 12–36 months to sell multifamily assets into a better market.
  • REIT II balance-sheet signal (company reports / Form 1-SA summaries through mid-2026): equity investments deployed on the order of ~$9.3–$9.5M across a handful of project interests; cash reported near zero in later periods (~$3,024 at year-end 2025; ~$177 in a mid-2026 snapshot cited in secondary coverage); book equity on the order of ~$4.7M at 31 December 2025 (~$4.12 per $10 share on independent arithmetic) with project carrying values described around half of cost — not a market NAV and not cash you can take out today.
  • Platform context (same DiversyFund / diversyfund.com file): four legacy retail REIT vehicles together raised on the order of ~$83M from tens of thousands of mostly non-accredited investors; none has returned original principal via a completed liquidation. Trustpilot https://www.trustpilot.com/review/diversyfund.com sits around Bad (~1.2 / 5 from ~677 reviews) on locked capital, missed timelines, portal failures and silence. Newer site products marketed to accredited investors at high minimums are a separate file from REIT II shares.
  • Anyone who offers to recover a DF Growth REIT II / DiversyFund balance for an upfront fee, or who asks for a seed phrase, a password, or a 2FA code, is attempting a second theft.

What is DF Growth REIT II (Growth REIT 2)?

Can I get my DF Growth REIT II money out now?
