The record

What the notices say

  • The plans are the verdict: “9% in 20 hours ($100–$900)”, “20% in 30 hours”, “50% in 72 hours”, “500% in 10 days ($20k–$80k)” with “deposit back at end of term” — guaranteed fixed returns no lawful investment can offer; the Ponzi hallmark (site reviewed 14 Oct 2026).
  • Its own counters are fabricated and mutually contradictory: “Total deposits $417,785,481.62” versus “Total withdrawals $986,389,179.79” — withdrawals 2.4 times deposits — alongside “1,801 total users” on “7,955 online days”.
  • Transnational burner branding: a Spanish brand name, a Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnam) address, and a language menu (EN/Vietnam/China/France/Italy) — no company registration, no licence number, anonymous operators.
  • The “6 years of hard work”, “$14M+ trading capital” and “110%+ average monthly profit” claims sit next to a raw-domain page title and a referral/representative programme — the recruitment layer that keeps a Ponzi breathing.
Loss or this pattern

A trading loss and this pattern are not the same

A trading loss is a real position that moved against you. You can see the trade. The pattern below is a different event.

  • a small starter plan ($100) that pays out at first — the Ponzi float
  • escalating tiers to $6k–$80k
  • withdrawals halting at thresholds
  • “representatives” recruiting through WhatsApp/Telegram communities — the channels Spain's CNMV repeatedly warns about
