The short answer: CryptoPocket is real — and the complaints against it are under investigation

CryptoPocket is a Spanish VASP-registered bitcoin broker with offices, a public team and a retail partner network. These are the facts that check out on public registers — the complaint record is what our review is still working through:

  • Bank of Spain VASP registration, with public offices and an identifiable management team.
  • A retail partner network across Spain and years of verifiable operation.

Checking out is not the same as closed: the complaints against CryptoPocket are under active investigation, and none of the facts above protects you from a fake. Most 'cryptopocket scam' searches trace to impersonators, not the platform itself — the ones that don't are the reason we investigate.

Sort your situation

Real-platform dispute or impersonation fraud?

Both feel identical from the inside. They are fixed by completely different routes — and the fastest way to tell them apart is to ask three questions.

If it is the real platform: your dispute runs through its formal complaints process, then the regulator or ombudsman. Slow, but it usually works. Small brokers are heavily impersonated because their support teams are hard to reach — which is exactly why scammers pose as them. The real CryptoPocket lives at cryptopocket.es; anything else is someone wearing its name.

If it was an impersonator: you were never a customer of CryptoPocket at all. A clone site, a fake “account manager”, a lookalike domain — the money went to criminals, not to the platform. That is fraud, and speed decides what can be recovered.

The three questions: did you type the official domain yourself, or follow a link, advert or message? Did anyone contact you first? Were you asked to pay a personal account or a different domain? If any answer is wrong, treat it as impersonation — and start here.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is CryptoPocket a scam?

No — CryptoPocket is a real business: Bank of Spain VASP registration, with public offices and an identifiable management team. The scam stories you see online usually trace to fakes borrowing its name — and the ones that don't are part of our active investigation.

Is CryptoPocket regulated?

Yes — Bank of Spain VASP registration, with public offices and an identifiable management team. You can verify it yourself on the public register of the named regulator.

I lost money to someone claiming to be CryptoPocket — is that the real company?

Almost certainly not. Impersonators borrow the name, the logo, even the licence number. The real company knows nothing about it. Keep every message, receipt, URL and wallet address — that evidence is what an investigation builds on.

I paid a fake CryptoPocket site. What is enough to send?

The domain you actually used, the date, and whether it was a card, a bank transfer, or crypto. Or the name of the person who told you to pay. Or one screenshot. We investigate losses as a cybersecurity company — we do not replace the bank, the card scheme, or the police. The first look at the file is free.

How do I check the CryptoPocket site I used is genuine?

Type the official domain yourself — cryptopocket.es — never follow a link from an advert, email or message. Then check the regulator's public register, which lists the firm's genuine website. If your domain differs by even one character, it was a fake.
