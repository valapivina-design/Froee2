Current status

What we observe at primedice.com today

As of October 2026, primedice.com is reported to be winding down — the operator has announced a shutdown and told users to withdraw by the deadline. There is no functioning platform, no support channel, and no one answering withdrawal requests under this name.

A legitimate operator in trouble publishes notices, appoints administrators and talks to its clients. A fraud operation simply goes dark. This is the dark pattern — and if the name comes back later as a “successor platform” contacting former users, treat it as the next phase of the same scheme, not a rescue. Put your energy into the trace instead: the money trail is the one asset that does not vanish with the website.

Copycat warning: TrustDice-style “PrimeDice” copycat pages already exist and ride the brand. Only primedice.com is the original operation. Any other domain presenting itself as a successor, mirror or “reactivated” Primedice — especially one that asks for a deposit or your credentials — should be treated as hostile.

Frequently asked questions

Is Primedice a scam?

Taking deposits and then vanishing — leaving reported to be winding down — the operator has announced a shutdown and told users to withdraw by the deadline — is the exit-scam pattern in full. Whether or not a regulator has formally listed it, the situation that matters is yours: the operator is gone, and the money trail is the only asset left.

What happened to the Primedice website?

Fraud platforms shut down when new deposits stop covering withdrawal demands — or when enforcement attention gets close. The domain gets parked, sold or left to rot. A vanished site is itself evidence.

Can I still recover money from Primedice?

Often, yes — the trail does not disappear with the website. Card and bank transfers may be recoverable through recall or dispute; crypto transactions are permanently recorded and traceable to exchanges. Speed and preserved evidence are the two things that matter.
